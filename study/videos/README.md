# CCAR-F Video Resources

Supplementary video links for the 30 [Concept Explainers](../content/ccarf-concepts.json) in the
study app — one Markdown file per exam domain, each with 1–3 videos per task statement
(1.1–5.6) that explain or demonstrate the concept.

These are curated **third-party** links, not part of the schema-validated `study/content/`
content the app reads, and are **not officially produced or endorsed by Anthropic** except
where a video is explicitly attributed to Anthropic's own channel below. Every link was
verified to resolve to a real, existing video at the time this was written — video
availability can change over time, so a stale link is possible later.

## How These Were Selected

For each concept, videos were prioritized in this order:

1. **YouTube** over other video platforms.
2. **Official/authoritative sources** — Anthropic's own channel first, then reputable
   channels that cover Claude/Anthropic tooling seriously.
3. **Hands-on demonstrations** (live coding, building an agent, configuring a real
   integration) over pure talking-head/slide explainers.

Where no video closely matched a narrow concept, the closest broader-topic video is
included instead and flagged as such in its note. A concept with no reasonable match at
all is marked `_No closely matching video found._` rather than padded with an irrelevant
link.
